Read only the supplied Hebrew or Aramaic sources. Return one compact JSON object matching the supplied schema. This is a first reading, not approval. Do not use biographies, registry IDs, outside sources, era labels, or dates. Do not create statement or event nodes. Write short reasons; one sentence normally suffices.

People: retain every named person, including fathers embedded in names. Also retain identifiable unnamed relatives, participants and groups in reported events. Keep these people even if there is no relationship. Anonymous generic actors in a legal hypothetical do not need separate person nodes; summarize them in episode.notes when useful. If included, they must remain hypothetical and never become historical connections. Use kind person/group/deity/angel/supernatural/not_a_person correctly. A generic legal actor is hypothetical, not a historical participant. Do not turn animals, objects, cited book titles, or collective voices into named people. An ambiguous name stays as written. Merge two mentions locally only when context supports it; say why. Person alternatives refer to other local person IDs, or null when the other identity cannot be identified. Never invent an extra person just to fill an endpoint.

Each source is given as a numbered list of units instead of plain text. Each unit is one word together with the spaces and punctuation that follow it; joined in order, the units are the exact source. Unit 0, if present, is text before the first word and cannot be chosen. Point at words with source_id, first and last: the numbers of the first and last unit of the words you mean, in one source, with first not after last. Never type or copy a quote and never count occurrences; the numbers are the anchor. A mention covers only the words of the units you choose, without the punctuation after the last word. Attached prefixes are part of a unit and are included automatically (the unit דאביי, the unit ורבנן). An abbreviation such as א"ר or אר"ש is one unit. Use the smallest identifying words, including a title when useful. Include every name occurrence, and enough pronouns/descriptions to identify unnamed participants. A father's name may overlap the child's full name: in רמי בר חמא the son is the units from רמי to חמא and the father is the unit חמא alone. If a father's name is only inside one abbreviated unit together with the son (for example רשב"ג), do not create a separate father person; mention the father in the son's reason. Relationship evidence is any range of units in one source. Do not assign the identical units to two person IDs unless explicit local alternatives connect them. Distinct pronouns can belong to different people. Labels may explain a local reading, but do not claim a global historical identity.

Relationships: record only text-supported relationships between the local people. Choose the relation from the supplied table. Do not return category or direction fields: they are derived from the selected relation during compilation. You must still choose subject and object in the order specified below. Give the concrete act or wording in verb and its reason/evidence. Keep an interaction when its outcome or one detail is uncertain. An attempted action is still an attempt, not a successful outcome. Speech containing a hypothetical legal case is a reported speech act; its imagined actors remain hypothetical. A rejected argument is still a debate. Polarity describes whether the action happened or was negated, not whether the people agree. A person actually opposing an argument has relation opposes and polarity positive. Use polarity negative only when the text says the specified action did not happen (for example, he did not answer). A failed attempt is a positive attempt, not a successful action. Use uncertain only when affirmation or negation of that action is unclear. Two people having similar views alone is not an interaction. Do not create links from co-occurrence alone.

For a response to a teaching, the object is its identifiable author and modality is response_to_teaching. In particular relation responds_to_teaching requires that modality, never asserted. This does not imply meeting or contemporaneity. If no author can be identified, object is null and reason explains the gap. Choose enough surrounding units as evidence to support that attribution. Direct speaking or answering uses asserted unless the speech itself is hypothetical or uncertain. scene_status belongs to each relationship: actual_report for an act reported by the text (not independently verified history), hypothetical for an imagined act, mixed only if the relationship itself spans both. Shmuel asking Rav about a hypothetical woman is actual_report between Shmuel and Rav; the woman is not an addressee and does not make their conversation hypothetical. Modality uncertain means the relationship or endpoint is unresolved, not merely that the historical event has not been independently verified.

Direction rules: child_of is child -> parent; child_in_law_of is spouse -> spouse's parent; student_of is student -> teacher; teaches is teacher -> student; reports_in_name_of is reporter -> named authority. spouse_of and sibling_of are symmetric; emit only one copy. For acts_on and speech/debate relations, subject acts or speaks and object receives the act or is the author of the teaching being answered. Family endpoints must be kind person. Do not infer a parent from a teacher or a direct encounter from attribution.

Ambiguous endpoints: leave the uncertain subject/object null. Preserve possible pairs in alternatives, with a reason for each. Do not assert every possible pair as a separate relationship. A fixed endpoint must remain fixed in every alternative. A null endpoint requires modality uncertain or response_to_teaching and an explanation. Both endpoints may be null if the action is grounded but neither participant can be resolved. Do not invent a named person to resolve a pronoun. A described unnamed participant with clear local reference may have its own person ID.

All IDs must be unique: people p1,p2,...; relationships r1,r2,... . Every referenced non-null ID must exist in people. No duplicate mentions within one person, duplicate alternatives, duplicate relationship/evidence entries, or inverse copies of symmetric relationships. Family links cannot connect someone to themselves. Every person and relationship needs a nonempty reason. Do not use a not_a_person entity as a relationship endpoint. A relationship involving a hypothetical actor must be hypothetical or uncertain. Each relationship needs at least one evidence range.
