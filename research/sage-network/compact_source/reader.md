Read only the supplied Hebrew or Aramaic sources. Return one compact JSON object matching the supplied schema. This is a first reading, not approval. Do not use biographies, registry IDs, outside sources, era labels, or dates. Do not create statement or event nodes. Write short reasons; one sentence normally suffices.

People: retain every named person, fathers embedded in names, unnamed participants, groups, and people described only through relatives. Keep everyone even if there is no relationship. Use kind person/group/deity/angel/supernatural/not_a_person correctly. A generic legal actor is hypothetical, not a historical participant. Do not turn animals, objects, cited book titles, or collective voices into named people. An ambiguous name stays as written. Merge two mentions locally only when context supports it; say why. Person alternatives refer to other local person IDs, or null when the other identity cannot be identified. Never invent an extra person just to fill an endpoint.

Each person's mentions are exact source occurrences: source_id, quote, occurrence (1-based among literal matches). Keep vowels and punctuation. Use the smallest identifying words, including a title when useful. Include every name occurrence, and enough pronouns/descriptions to identify unnamed participants. A father's name may overlap the child's full name. Mentions must not start or end inside a Hebrew word: include attached prefixes in the exact quote (for example quote דר״ע or ורב when printed that way). Never take רבא out of תרבא or לה out of בלילה. General relationship evidence may be any exact substring. Do not assign the identical name occurrence to two person IDs unless explicit local alternatives connect them. Distinct pronouns can belong to different people. Do not expand an abbreviation inside a quote. Labels may explain a local reading, but do not claim a global historical identity.

Relationships: record only text-supported relationships between the local people. Category is action/family/teaching/debate. Use the supplied relation table for category and direction. Give the concrete act or wording in verb and its reason/evidence. Keep an interaction when its outcome or one detail is uncertain. An attempted action is still an attempt, not a successful outcome. Speech containing a hypothetical legal case is a reported speech act; its imagined actors remain hypothetical. A rejected argument is still a debate. Two people having similar views alone is not an interaction. Do not create links from co-occurrence alone.

For a response to a teaching, the object is its identifiable author and modality is response_to_teaching. In particular relation responds_to_teaching requires that modality, never asserted. This does not imply meeting or contemporaneity. If no author can be identified, object is null and reason explains the gap. Quote enough surrounding evidence to support that attribution. Direct speaking or answering uses asserted unless the speech itself is hypothetical or uncertain. scene_status belongs to each relationship: actual_report for an act reported by the text (not independently verified history), hypothetical for an imagined act, mixed only if the relationship itself spans both. Shmuel asking Rav about a hypothetical woman is actual_report between Shmuel and Rav; the woman is not an addressee and does not make their conversation hypothetical. Modality uncertain means the relationship or endpoint is unresolved, not merely that the historical event has not been independently verified.

Direction rules: child_of is child -> parent; child_in_law_of is spouse -> spouse's parent; student_of is student -> teacher; teaches is teacher -> student; reports_in_name_of is reporter -> named authority. spouse_of and sibling_of are symmetric; emit only one copy. For acts_on and speech/debate relations, subject acts or speaks and object receives the act or is the author of the teaching being answered. Family endpoints must be kind person. Do not infer a parent from a teacher or a direct encounter from attribution.

Ambiguous endpoints: leave the uncertain subject/object null. Preserve possible pairs in alternatives, with a reason for each. Do not assert every possible pair as a separate relationship. A fixed endpoint must remain fixed in every alternative. A null endpoint requires modality uncertain or response_to_teaching and an explanation. Both endpoints may be null if the action is grounded but neither participant can be resolved. Do not invent a named person to resolve a pronoun. A described unnamed participant with clear local reference may have its own person ID.

All IDs must be unique: people p1,p2,...; relationships r1,r2,... . Every referenced non-null ID must exist in people. No duplicate mentions within one person, duplicate alternatives, duplicate relationship/evidence entries, or inverse copies of symmetric relationships. Family links cannot connect someone to themselves. Every person and relationship needs a nonempty reason. Do not use a not_a_person entity as a relationship endpoint. A relationship involving a hypothetical actor must be hypothetical or uncertain. Each relationship needs at least one exact evidence anchor.

Read all supplied sources. When a dependency needs missing text, coverage is needs_context and needed_context lists what to fetch or which question is unresolved. Otherwise use complete_for_supplied_text and an empty needed_context list. Uncertainty that cannot be fixed by more text can instead be explained in reasons/notes.
