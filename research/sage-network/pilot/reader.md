# Read one supplied episode

Read the Hebrew or Aramaic in the job's sources. Return one JSON object conforming exactly to schema.json. Use job_id from the job and schema_version passage-pilot-v1. Do not use prior pair labels, case reviews, review rubrics, biographies or outside sources. These are first-pass readings, not final historical graph edges.

Read all supplied sources. Keep every identifiable participant, including women named through relationships, unnamed speakers, groups, pronouns and people embedded in patronymics. A mention is one occurrence of exact words. Entities are LOCAL referents within this episode, not global historical identities. Make separate entities when a reading changes their type or identity. A family path may need an unnamed intermediate relative.

Anchors contain source_id, an EXACT nonempty substring of that source text, and occurrence (1-based among literal matches). Keep vowels, punctuation and brackets. Do not normalize quotes, insert ellipses or use a translation as Hebrew evidence. The compiler calculates character offsets. Entity evidence must support its presence or meaning; statements and events also need anchors. Repeated pronouns can have distinct mention IDs. Uncertain pronouns belong in coreference_candidates; do not silently settle them.

Save multiple claims when the wording supports them. Every claim has evidence, direction, voice and reading scope. Use predicates from contract.py. Keep uncommon actions as event entities and participates_in claims; put the original verb/action and role in subtype, roles and notes. Do not create an edge merely because two names occur together. Do not create duplicate inverse edges. child_of's subject is the child; its object is the parent. A quotation does not prove direct hearing, teaching, co-presence, age order or calendar dates.

A speech act can be directed to a person while answering a statement. Use statement/event nodes and roles (addressee, content, target, participant, location, etc.) to retain both. rules_like needs a content role pointing to the SPECIFIC statement. Do not turn question, supposition, prediction, dream or rejected suggestion into asserted fact. voice is narrator, unknown, or an entity ID; quote nesting should be explained in notes. A speech act that really occurs can be asserted even when its content is a question or a prediction; encode the content and explain this distinction.

Give each claim all required fields. identity_confidence and historical_confidence are null: this pass does not assess them. reading_confidence is high/medium/low for THIS reading. It is not a calibrated probability. basis is explicit, local_coreference or interpretation. subtype and time_scope may be null. Use notes for explanation, especially uncertainty, compound relations, attribution and uncommon actions.

Genre is halachic, aggadic, mixed or unknown. Explain the choice. Genre is not proof that events happened. form can describe legal argument, teaching chain, story, dream, parable or a mixture. Mark needs_context when supplied passages cannot settle a dependency; say which text or question is missing. Never fill it from memory.

Represent explicit alternative reports, editorial uncertainty and ambiguous readings with reading_groups. Branch IDs are globally unique within the job, and claims/entities name the branches where they hold. Empty branches means shared across all readings. Two IDs from the SAME group mean either branch; IDs from DIFFERENT groups mean both conditions. Every branch must have at least one scoped claim or entity. Keep source alternatives even if both could have happened. historical_compatibility normally remains unknown. Explain branch scope and where it rejoins. Nested alternatives can use multiple group memberships; explain dependent combinations in notes and leave them unresolved when necessary. Do not duplicate shared claims into competing branches.

Do not silently omit a difficult relationship. Keep grounded alternatives or an open question. Zero claims is valid when the text supplies none; explain it in episode.notes. Completeness means only that the supplied text was read; it does not certify that every possible claim was found.
