# original-03: Chullin 94b:13, a meeting on the road

The Gemara tells this story to back up a rule: people who mislead themselves are not being misled (94b:12). It is told in the narrator's voice. No participant tells it as his own memory. The story runs on into 94b:14.

## What happens, step by step

| # | What | Who → to whom | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mar Zutra son of Rav Nachman goes from Sikhra to Bei Mechoza | Mar Zutra | narrated action |
| 2 | Rava and Rav Safra come towards Sikhra, travelling as one party | Rava + Rav Safra | narrated action |
| 3 | "They met each other" (פגעו אהדדי) | all three | **actual meeting** |
| 4 | Mar Zutra thinks they have come out to greet him | Mar Zutra | **narrated thought, and it is wrong** |
| 5 | "Why did the Rabbis trouble themselves so much?" | Mar Zutra → Rava and Rav Safra (spoken to both at once) | speech built on the misunderstanding |
| 6 | "We did not know you were coming; **if we had known, we would have troubled more**" | Rav Safra → Mar Zutra | fact + **counterfactual** |
| 7 | "Why did you tell him? You upset him" | Rava → Rav Safra (Mar Zutra is the subject, not the one addressed) | rebuke (94b:14) |
| 8 | "But [otherwise] we would be misleading him" | Rav Safra → Rava (speaker not named in the text) | objection that rests on a condition (if they stayed silent) |
| 9 | "He is misleading himself" | Rava → Rav Safra (**one reading**; see below) | conclusion, later adopted into Jewish law |

**Who meets:** all three men meet in person. Rava and Rav Safra are travelling companions. Mar Zutra is going the other way. The ו ("and") in the first-pass pattern joins two travel clauses. It does not make Mar Zutra and Rava companions.

**Speech that is imagined, conditional or remembered:**
- Mar Zutra's belief that they came for him is a thought, not speech.
- Rav Safra's "we would have troubled more" describes something that never happened.
- "We would be misleading him" depends on a condition: if they had stayed silent.
- No speech in the story is remembered. The whole incident is the narrator's citation.
- Rava never speaks to Mar Zutra.

**No honour edge.** Commentators agree the two were not coming for Mar Zutra at all. Rashi says they were on their own business. Ritva says they were going their own way. Tosafot say they did not come for him at all.

## Where the sources differ

- **Who says the last line.** The Aramaic has a single "he said to him" covering both sentences. The Davidson English and Steinsaltz Hebrew give the last line to Rava, but they are one editorial project, so they count as one voice. The Rif's text adds a second explicit "he said to him". Petach Einayim says Rava concludes and Rav Safra accepts. Tur and Shulchan Arukh (Choshen Mishpat 228:6) make the line the rule.
- **Place.** The Rif reads *Bei Chozai*, has Rava and Rav Safra coming *from* there, and lacks the "he thought" clause. This is a text variant. Manuscripts were not checked.
- **What happened afterwards.** Ben Yehoyada infers that they turned back with Mar Zutra to Mechoza and hosted him. That is his inference, not an event in the text.
- **Mar Zutra's knowledge.** Rashi: nobody told him they came for him. Rabbeinu Gershom: "he knows" they did not come out to meet him.
- **A different story.** A later version, cited from Reshit Chokhmah by Petach Einayim and also reported by Seder HaDorot, has Rav Safra, an unnamed pious man and students. It is a separate episode, not the same people.
- **Proposed emendation.** Haggahot Ya'avetz proposes changing Rav Safra's wording. His exact intent is unclear.

## Proposed graph changes (all provisional)

- **Mar Zutra – Rava:** replace "together" with "met on the road". Mar Zutra speaks to Rava as one of a pair. Include the title מר in the name span.
- **Rava – Rav Safra:** replace "together" with "travelled together". Add a separate exchange from 94b:14: Rava rebukes Rav Safra, Rav Safra objects, Rava answers.
- **Add Mar Zutra – Rav Safra:** met on the road. Rav Safra replies to Mar Zutra and corrects his mistake. This is the only speech anyone addresses to Mar Zutra.
- **Add kinship:** Mar Zutra is the child of a local placeholder "Rav Nachman", taken from the name "son of Rav Nachman". Which historical Rav Nachman this is has not been decided.
- **Do not add** any honour or greeting edge towards Mar Zutra.
