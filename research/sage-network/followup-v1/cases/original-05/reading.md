# Arakhin 20b:9 — one question, several versions

Status: researched. Every graph proposal here is provisional. Full evidence is in
`dossier.json`. Saved sources are in `sources/`, with URLs and hashes in
`sources/fetch_log.json`. Translations are this dossier's own short renderings.

## What the text says

A baraita appears to let someone consecrate money they have not yet received.
The Gemara objects that "a person cannot consecrate something that has not come
into the world" (20b:8). The answer is given in nested versions:

1. **Placement** (20b:10, "ואיכא דמתני לה אהא", "some teach it on this"): the
   discussion is attached either to the baraita about the proceeds of a bull
   (20b:6-9) or to the baraita about renting out a house (20b:10-21a:4). Rashi
   says the whole unit moves: "להא דרב יהודה ואביי ורמי בר חמא" ("that of Rav
   Yehuda and Abaye and Rami bar Hama").
2. **Form**: either Rav Yehuda reports in Rav's name that the baraita is Rabbi
   Meir's view (20b:8), or ("איכא דאמרי", "some say") the answer came as a
   question and reply (20b:9).
3. **Speaker pair** inside the exchange ("ואמרי לה", "and some say it"): Rav
   Pappa asks Abaye, **or** Rami bar Hama asks Rav Hisda.

The reply "אֲמַר לֵיהּ: וְאֶלָּא כְּמַאן?" ("He said to him: Rather, like
whom?") names no speaker. The person who was asked answers: Abaye in one pair,
Rav Hisda in the other. At 20b:9 the Davidson English adds "Abaye", so it
follows only the first pair. At the repeat in 21a:4 the same translation says
"He said to him". Steinsaltz at 21a:4 says "הנשאל" ("the one asked"). Both
come from the same editorial project, so they are not independent witnesses.

## Corrections to the input pairs

- **Rav Pappa asks Abaye**: keep this, but scope it to the exchange version and
  the first speaker pair.
- **Abaye – Rav Hisda "none"**: correct. They are alternative people asked in
  different versions, not people who spoke to each other. The first-pass label
  "addresses" was wrong.
- **Rav Hisda "follows" Rabbi Meir**: remove. "כמאן" asks which view an
  anonymous baraita follows. The exchange assigns the baraita to Rabbi Meir's
  legal view. It says nothing about Rav Hisda's own loyalty or rulings.
- **Missing**: Rami bar Hama asks Rav Hisda (second pair). Rav Yehuda reports
  in the name of Rav (report version). Rami bar Hama is son of a Hama (the
  relation is carried by the name, so keep a local placeholder for the father).

## Repeated passage and name variant

21a:4 repeats 20b:8-9 under the other placement. It is the **same
tradition**, not a second observation, so it adds no weight to any edge. At
21a:4 the printed Hebrew (Davidson vocalized, Davidson unvocalized, Wikisource)
reads **רב מרי בר חמא**. At 20b:9 it reads **רמי בר חמא**. Rashash corrects 21a
to "רמי בר חמא ... כצ"ל כדלעיל" ("so it should read, as above"). That is a later
proposed correction, kept separate from the printed reading. No manuscript was
checked. The printed form stays as printed. Merging the two names is a
provisional proposal.
