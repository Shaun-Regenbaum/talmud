# Keep the anonymous opinion and the named explanation apart

An unattributed Mishnah opinion, Yose’s view, and Hanina’s explanation transmitted by Yaakov are distinct claims. An explanation of an older view is not a meeting with its author.

Keep the source-specific number and uncertain speaker. A later manuscript report is not direct manuscript inspection.

The chronological emendation to Ami and the proposal of an earlier Mana are different ways to read the difficulty. Neither is a settled identity or a basis to rewrite the printed text.

Keep both parent placeholders without merging the two Buns. Preserve the partner ruling’s negation by edition; opposite wording cannot be combined into one unqualified rule.

No historical identity or graph edge is approved.
