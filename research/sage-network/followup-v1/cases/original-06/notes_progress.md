# Progress notes (original-06, Arakhin 13b:4)

Saved early so an interruption keeps the findings. The dossier is the final record.

1. Three digital editions of the Bavli line (Koren vocalized, Koren unvocalized, Wikisource/Vilna-based)
   agree word for word on the attribution: אמר רב הונא ואמרי לה אמר רב זבדי אמר רב הונא.
   They are not independent witnesses: the two Koren texts are one editorial work, and all three follow the printed tradition.
2. Both routes end in Rav Huna. Route A: Rav Huna said. Route B: Rav Zavdi said that Rav Huna said.
   The teaching (up to 120 trumpets, from II Chr 5:12) is identical in both.
3. The Mishnah (Arakhin 2:5, Torat Emet text) reads "ומוסיפין עד לעולם" for trumpets; the Gemara's "ועד כמה" limits it.
4. Tosafot (Vilna) on 13b:4 quotes "אמר רב הונא ואמרי לה כו'" and explains only the halakhic content (two explanations). It says nothing about which route is right.
5. Steinsaltz Hebrew (same Koren editorial project) glosses ואמרי לה as "ויש ד'דאומרים" and keeps both.
6. Mishnat Eretz Yisrael (Kvutsat Yavne) quotes both routes and calls the statement a derasha on a past event, not a tradition.
7. Rashi on Arakhin 13b in the Sefaria Vilna text has no comment on segment 4 (checked only this amud's Sefaria segmentation).
8. German Goldschmidt version request returned a warning (wrong language key). Not a finding about content.
