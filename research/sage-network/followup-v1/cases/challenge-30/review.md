# Keep the ban target separate from the addressee

The base text supports a father as the target of the ban. It leaves the person
spoken to unstated. Other saved versions add “to him”. Keep both readings and
their source words.

The three-year duration comes from an attributed precedent in the wider
conversation. The later release comes from commentary. Keep those voices visible.

The related Yerushalmi story does not justify merging its people with these
people. It can still belong in an evidence pack comparing the two tellings.
