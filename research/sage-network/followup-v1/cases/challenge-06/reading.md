# Menachot 73b:16: who taught the "home-born" baraita?

**Status:** researched. Full evidence is in [dossier.json](dossier.json). Source files are in `sources/`.

## What the segment says

The anonymous Talmud voice gives two answers to "who is the tanna?" of an anonymous baraita. The baraita says a gentile does not bring libations by themselves, but his burnt offering needs them.

- **Rabbi Yosei HaGelili**, if one deletes "wine" (סמי מההיא יין) from his earlier baraita.
- **Rabbi Akiva**, if his "only a burnt offering" means "a burnt offering and all its companions".

The only named people in 73b:12-16 are Rabbi Shimon (the Mishnah's speaker), Rabbi Yosei HaGelili and Rabbi Akiva. No family relation appears.

## What wider context changes

1. **The deleted word is in 73b:8.** That list of offerings closes with "the words of Rabbi Yosei HaGelili" (73b:9). Rashi and Rabbeinu Gershom give the corrected wording: "birds and frankincense", without wine. The deletion is a correction that the Talmud proposes to a transmitted text. It is not a manuscript reading, and it does not say that Yosei changed his mind (F01, F02).
2. **The Yosei "wine" view needs a branch condition.** The first reading saved it as true in every reading, but the Yosei answer withdraws it (F03).
3. **Parallel texts swap the names.** In the saved Sifra (Venice 1545), the inclusive list belongs to Rabbi Akiva, and Rabbi Yosei HaGelili answers "only a burnt offering". Lieberman reports the same swap in the Erfurt and London manuscripts of the Tosefta. The Bavli follows the Vienna manuscript and the first printed edition. Every answer in 73b:12-16 depends on the Bavli's order (F06).
4. **The Tosefta's list has no wine.** The Vienna text of the Tosefta names meal offerings and salt but not wine, and its Akiva allows burnt and peace offerings. This is parallel-witness evidence beside the proposed deletion, not proof of it (F07).
5. **"Said to him" is a speech turn only in the parallels.** Its direction changes between witnesses, and the London manuscript seems to lack it. The Bavli just lists the two views. There is no meeting or addressed speech in 73b:16 (F08).
6. **"And this is one of them" points to Shekalim 7:5.** The antecedent is the found animal's libations, not the gentile rule (F09).
7. **Agreement is not authorship.** "In accordance with whom…" asks whether Rabbi Shimon's mishnah agrees with a sage's view. Claims c5, c6 and c10 should not say `attributes_to` (F10).
8. **Temurah 2b gives the same rule to "Rabbi Shimon"**, with the same "home-born" baraita as its source (Temurah 3a). A shared name does not make him the Mishnah's Rabbi Shimon (F11).
9. **Earlier speakers are outside the pilot window.** Rav Huna, Rav Chama bar Gurya, Rava, Rav Sheizevi and Rabbi Yochanan speak in 73a:16-73b:5. "bar Gurya" supports a father placeholder, Gurya, if the window is widened (F12).
