# Keep the view separate from the person

The wider passage ties the position label to Rebbi. Keep the view being discussed and the anonymous question or answer separate from an utterance by Rebbi.

An unnamed first opinion should not become a historical group merely because the introduction says the Rabbis taught. A later authority calls the side the Rabbis. Save that attribution separately and leave its actual membership unknown.

A commentator identifies the target as an account of the dispute. This is not evidence that the named people were in a conversation. Competing named attributions remain source branches.

Keep the printed cross-reference as data about that edition and the different identification supported by the commentaries. Do not silently rewrite the saved source.

No historical identity or graph edge is approved.
