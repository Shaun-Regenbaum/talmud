# Pesachim 103a:5: Rav Huna bar Yehuda at Rava's house

Status: researched. All graph proposals are provisional and local to this passage.
The full record, with exact quotes and hashes, is in `dossier.json`.

## What happens (103a:5-8)

1. **Visit.** "רב הונא בר יהודה איקלע לבי רבא". Rav Huna bar Yehuda comes to
   Rava's house.
2. **Objects brought.** "אייתו לקמייהו מאור ובשמים". Unnamed members of the
   household bring light and spices "before them". The Rif and the Rosh read
   the singular "לקמיה" ("before him").
3. **Action.** Rava blesses over the spices first, then over the light. The
   spices and light are objects, not people.
4. **Challenge.** "אמר ליה: והא בין בית שמאי ובין בית הילל מאור ברישא". The
   speaker is not named at the verb. He is Rav Huna: the next named turn is
   Rava's, Rashbam says so ("רב הונא לרבא לאחר שגמר הבדלה"), and the Rosh's
   quotation adds "רב הונא". The addressee is Rava.
5. **Source for the challenge.** In Pesachim the anonymous Gemara supplies it
   ("ומאי היא? דתנן"), citing Mishnah Berakhot 8:5. In the Berakhot 52b parallel,
   Rav Huna cites it himself.
6. **Reply.** "עני רבא בתריה". Rava says the mishnah is Rabbi Meir's version.
   He quotes Rabbi Yehuda's version, in which Beit Hillel put spices before
   light.
7. **Ruling.** "ואמר רבי יוחנן נהגו העם כבית הילל ואליבא דרבי יהודה". The text
   does not mark whether Rava or the editor adds this.

## Roles kept apart

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Visitor, challenger (inferred) | Rav Huna bar Yehuda (P1) |
| Host, performs the blessing, addressee, replies | Rava (P2) |
| Silent parent in the name | Yehuda, father of P1 (P3) |
| Agents who bring the items | unnamed household (group) |
| Objects of the action | spices, light |
| Cited authorities | Beit Shammai, Beit Hillel (schools); R. Meir and R. Yehuda (cited by Rava); R. Yohanan (citer uncertain) |

R. Yehuda the tanna is not Yehuda, Rav Huna's father. They only share a name.

## Do later segments change who is present?

No. 103a:7 names Rava as the answerer, which confirms the addressee.
103a:9 is a separate visit to Rava by Rav Ya'akov bar Abba, about wine
blessings. Rashbam places it on Shabbat itself and the Rav Huna visit on an
ordinary Saturday night. Keep the two episodes and their visitors apart.

## Name variant

The Rif on Pesachim reads "רב הונא ברבי יהודה". The WD and Wikisource Talmud
texts, the Rif on Berakhot, the Rosh and Halakhot Gedolot read "בר יהודה".
Keep both forms on P1 and keep the father relation. Seder HaDorot lists both
visits under Rav Huna bar Yehuda. It wonders whether there were two men of
that name. That is a historical question for a later identity check. No
manuscripts were checked.

## Halakhic context

Rava's order, spices before light, follows R. Yohanan's report of custom. The
codes adopt it:

- Halakhot Gedolot 2:6 cites this story.
- Rambam, Shabbat 29:24: wine, spices, candle, havdala.
- Tur and Shulchan Arukh OC 296:1: "יבנ"ה".

Putting wine first comes from the codes, not from this passage. Tosafot notes
that R. Yohanan here departs from an anonymous mishnah.

## Corrections to earlier pair judgments

- **Rav Huna bar Yehuda / Rava (103a:5, 0-27).** The first-pass label "before"
  is wrong. "איקלע לבי" means he visited; it is not a student sitting before a
  teacher. Keep "addresses" (Rav Huna to Rava), but base it on "אמר ליה" plus
  the speaker identification above. Add Rava's reply to Rav Huna (from 103a:7).
- **Rava / Rava (103a:5, 27-62).** "Same person" is correct. Drop "before".
- **Missing entries.** Add the father placeholder Yehuda (a relation embedded in
  the name) and the cited authorities.
- **Double counting.** The Berakhot 52b telling is the same event. It should not
  add weight to the Rav Huna and Rava edge.
