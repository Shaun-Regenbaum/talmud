3. 103a:7-8 "עני רבא בתריה": Rava answers with R. Meir vs R. Yehuda baraita, plus
   R. Yohanan: "נהגו העם כבית הילל ואליבא דרבי יהודה". Later segment confirms Rava is the
   addressee of the challenge; does not change participant identity.
4. Parallel Berakhot 52b:24-26: same visit, "חזייה לרבא", "מכדי ... אמאור לא פליגי", "דתניא"
   (WD) vs Rif Berakhot "דתנן". Rashbam on Pesachim: "ולא פליגי לא גרסינן" (rejects that wording here).
5. Rif Pesachim 20b:5 reads "רב הונא ברבי יהודה" and "אייתו לקמיה" (singular) and "פתח רבא".
   Name variant -> identity question (bar Yehuda vs b'Rabbi/Rav Yehuda). Rif Berakhot 39a reads "בר יהודה".
6. Seder HaDorot 995 lists both Pesachim 103a and Berakhot 52b under Rav Huna bar Yehuda and
   ends by doubting whether there were two people of that name (historical, not textual).
