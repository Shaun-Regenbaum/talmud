# Working notes (preserved in case of interruption)

- Sefaria texts (Wikisource, William Davidson Aramaic, vocalized) agree on 29b:3-5 wording. Printed "חזור [לאחורך]" in 29b:5: the bracketed word is absent in WD Aramaic ("אמר לו חזור חזר לאחוריו"); Ein Yaakov (Daat) reads "א״ל חזור לאחוריך".
- 29b:4: teacher is NOT named. "כיון שהגיע לדבר אחד" has no explicit subject; "תלמידיו" = his students. Akiva identity = story coreference to "הראהו לי" (show him). WD adds "Rabbi Akiva" in plain text; Steinsaltz same editorial work.
- Rashi 29b:4:2: "נתיישבה דעתו - של משה הואיל ומשמו אומר" — resolves "his mind" to Moses; says Akiva speaks in Moses's name.
- Rashi 29b:5:1 links the weighing of flesh to Berakhot 61b (combed with iron combs). Romans named only by WD/Steinsaltz plain text. Community translation renders "flaying his flesh with [iron] combs" — a translation shaped by Rashi/Berakhot, not a text variant.
- Ben Yehoyada: Ein Yaakov reads "לסוף שמונה עשרה שורות". CORRECTED: Sefaria Daat Ein Yaakov (vocalized) reads "שמונה (עשרה) שורות"; the Glick Ein Yaakov Hebrew reads "לסוף שמונה שורות" (my first note wrongly gave the Glick Hebrew as Daat). Seder HaDorot paraphrase reads "לסוף י"ח שורות".
- Ben Yehoyada: Moses saw the SOULS of Akiva and his students before birth (interpretation of the scene's time status).
- Maharsha reads "כיון שהגיעו" (plural, they reached) in his gloss; interprets "דבר אחד" as the alef/unity.
- Marit HaAyin / Petach Einayim: kabbalistic soul-root claims (Akiva a spark of Jacob; root of Issachar, of Cain). Not kinship.
- Shabbat 89a:5: same opening scene "בשעה שעלה משה למרום מצאו להקב"ה שהיה קושר כתרים לאותיות" attributed to Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, different dialogue. Separate chain.
- Berakhot 61b:10: "זו תורה וזו שכרה" said by the ministering angels about Akiva's death; students also address him there.
- Seder HaDorot also cites Avodah Zarah 5a: God showed Adam each generation; rejoiced at Akiva's Torah, grieved at his death.
