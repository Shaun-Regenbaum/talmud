# Save the words that were not said

The passage says Yirmiya did not use the formal address “Master”. That absence
is part of its argument that he was a student-colleague. Save it as negative
speech evidence, not as a spoken line.

Later sources quote different wording and sometimes different people. Keep
those versions alongside the focal text. Their disagreements belong in a future
identity pack for Yirmiya bar Abba.

The legal scope of “student-colleague” differs across later authorities. Keep
each explanation attributed. No historical person merge or date is approved.
