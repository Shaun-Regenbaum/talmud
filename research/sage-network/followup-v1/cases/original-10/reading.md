# Pesachim 103a:7: reading (original-10)

All proposals are provisional. Finding IDs refer to `dossier.json`. Translations are the researcher's own.

## The exchange (103a:5-8)

1. **Practice performed (103a:5).** Rav Huna bar Yehuda comes to Rava's house. Rava blesses the spices first, then the light.
2. **Live challenge (103a:5-6).** Rav Huna bar Yehuda objects: "both Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel put light first". The Mishnah (Berakhot 8:5) is quoted as the source ("ומאי היא? דתנן"). In Pesachim it is unclear whether the challenger or the anonymous Gemara supplies it. At Berakhot 52b the citation is inside the challenger's own words (F07).
3. **Response (103a:7).** "עני רבא בתריה": Rava answers him. First, he assigns the Mishnah's version to **Rabbi Meir** ("זו דברי רבי מאיר"). Rashbam explains this by the rule that an anonymous Mishnah follows Rabbi Meir. Second, he quotes **Rabbi Yehuda's** version: the schools disagreed only over light and spices, with Beit Hillel putting spices first (F03-F05). The Tosefta preserves this teaching under Rabbi Yehuda's name, with no Rabbi Meir mentioned (F06).
4. **Reported custom (103a:8).** Rabbi Yochanan: "the people follow Beit Hillel according to Rabbi Yehuda". It is unclear whether Rava is quoting him or the anonymous Gemara adds it. The English "Rava acted as dictated by this custom" is the translator's addition. It follows Steinsaltz and has no Aramaic counterpart (F09).

The passage's remembered practice in the strict sense is a separate episode at 103a:9. There Rava answers a different visitor, Rav Yaakov bar Abba, with "when we were at the Exilarch's house, this is what we did" (F12).

## Relations

- **Meeting:** Rava and Rav Huna bar Yehuda only, in a narrated visit. The Berakhot 52b telling is a parallel version of the same encounter, not a second meeting.
- **Rava -> Rabbi Meir:** `attributes_text_to`. Not a dispute and not a meeting.
- **Rava -> Rabbi Yehuda:** `cites_teaching`. Not a meeting.
- **Rabbi Meir <-> Rabbi Yehuda:** a dispute over *what the schools disputed*. Rabbi Meir's side comes from Rava's attribution. No exchange between them is shown.
- **Rabbi Yehuda -> Beit Shammai and -> Beit Hillel:** `reports_position_of`, for both schools, plus the dispute Beit Shammai <-> Beit Hillel.
- **Name kinship:** Rav Huna bar Yehuda is `child_of` a local placeholder "Yehuda" (literal patronymic).

## Alternative reported wording

- **Berakhot 52b:** "חזייה... מכדי... לא פליגי, דתניא", and "עני רבא בתריה" without "ואמר". Rashbam on Pesachim rejects a reading "ולא פליגי" there. I infer, without proof, that the rejected reading resembles the Berakhot wording (F02, F07).
- **Jerusalem Talmud Berakhot 8:5:** Rabbi Yehuda's teaching with the schools **reversed**: Beit Shammai put spices first. It is followed by Rabbi Ba and Rav Yehuda in the name of Rav: the law follows whoever says spices first. The Venice, Mechon-Mamre and Guggenheimer texts all read this way (F08). The Bavli/Tosefta and Yerushalmi readings are separate version branches; neither is treated as the correction of the other.
- **Rashi on Berakhot 52b:** "וא"ר יוחנן – גרסי'" signals that a variant existed, but the variant itself is unknown (F10).
