# Interim notes (challenge-12, Sanhedrin 97a:6-7)

Saved as soon as found; the dossier is the final record.

1. Person of the narration in 97a:7 differs by edition. Wikisource (Vilna): סבר / אמר לה (he thought / he said to her). Koren vocalized Aramaic: סְבַרִי / אֲמַרִי לַהּ (I thought / I said to her), but still שְׁכִיבוּ לֵיהּ תְּרֵין בְּנֵיהּ and לְקַמֵּיהּ (his sons / before him). Ein Yaakov (Glick Hebrew): איקלע (3rd) but נסיבי / הוו לי (1st), סבר / אמר לה. Maharal quotes 3rd person throughout (אקלע, נסיב, הוי ליה).
2. Chokhmat Shlomo: "אמר ליה ההוא כו' צ"ל אמר לי" — some text read "said to him"; he emends to "said to me". Maharal quotes both forms and builds on אמר ליה in his allegorical reading.
3. Bava Metzia 49a:16 parallel: Rav Papi < Ravina < "a sage named Rav Tavut, some say Rav Shmuel bar Zutra", same honesty formula, different story (sesame). Be'er Sheva 13 proposes emending BM to Rav Tavyomi per Sanhedrin (a later emendation, not a variant). Gilyon HaShas repeats it. Seder HaDorot discusses chronology.
4. Rashi: the knock = the neighbor was asking for the wife (שהיתה שואלת אותה); improper to say where she is since washing her hair. Rashi lemma reads אבבא, not אדשא.
5. Davidson English plain words "Since he deviated from the truth" = editorial causal link; bold text only juxtaposes lie and deaths. Residents' request (ולא תגרי בהו מותנא) implies the link inside the story.
6. Reshit Chokhmah retells: Rav Tavyomi "buried two sons and they expelled him" (וגירשוהו) — later retelling; text has only a request.
7. Glick English: "I consequently met a certain Rabbi named Tabuth" — translation adds "met"; text has "said to me".
8. Female neighbor is "her neighbor" (שיבבתה) — neighbor of the wife: missed social relation in first reading.
