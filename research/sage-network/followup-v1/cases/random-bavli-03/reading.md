# Sotah 45a:13: who is named, what is transmitted, what is disputed

Job `random-bavli-03`. Status: researched. Details, exact quotes and hashes are in
[dossier.json](dossier.json). Saved sources are in [sources/](sources/).

## The passage in one paragraph

The mishnah (44b:11) says no heifer is broken for a slain person found hidden in
a heap. The Gemara (45a:10) asks whether this follows only Rabbi Yehuda. A
baraita has Rabbi Yehuda exclude a *hidden* forgotten sheaf ("בשדה – פרט
לטמון") and the Sages include it. Rav (45a:11) answers that it can follow even
the Rabbis, because each verse is read in its own context. In the focal line
(45a:13) the Gemara asks why Rabbi Yehuda does not also use Rav's harvest
comparison, and concedes that he does: "אין הכי נמי". Rabbi Yehuda's "בשדה" is then
reassigned to standing grain (45a:14). His "בשדך" goes to the teaching that
**Rabbi Abahu transmits from Rabbi Elazar**: sheaves blown into a fellow's field
are not "forgotten" (45a:15).

## Repeated mentions the first reading missed

In the input 45a:11-15 there are 11 explicit name or group mentions. The first
reading anchored 8. It missed these three, all in 45a:15:
- the second **רבי אבהו** (in "דאמר רבי אבהו", after the first inside "לכדרבי אבהו");
- the second **ר' יהודה** (in "ור' יהודה בשדה בשדך לא משמע ליה");
- **רבנן** (in "ורבנן מבשדה בשדך").

Pronouns carry the rest: ליה refers to Rabbi Yehuda at 45a:13, 45a:14, and twice at 45a:15, and
the two להו refer to the Rabbis at 45a:14. Two typing fixes: m9 רבנן is a group, not a name,
and "תיפוק ליה" (m6) is a question with a pronoun, not a group mention.

The Abahu-to-Elazar chain is named **three times**: twice in 45a:15 and once when Rav
Kahana cites it at 45a:17. That is one transmission with three mentions, not three
observations. Wikisource abbreviates the second name as ר"א. The Davidson Aramaic spells
out רבי אלעזר, so this is a spelling difference between editions, not a variant
attribution.

## The explanatory link that was lost

"ור' יהודה מיבעי ליה לכדרבי אבהו אמר ר' אלעזר" is the unnamed Talmudic voice
saying which teaching Rabbi Yehuda's verse serves. Rashi gives the source word as
"בשדך ולא בשדה חבירך". This links an exegesis to a teaching. It is **not** a
relationship between Rabbi Yehuda and Rabbi Abahu or Rabbi Elazar. He does not cite them,
and the text asserts no contact.

## Transmission versus disagreement about the law

| Item | Kind | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden forgotten sheaf (45a:10) | **Legal disagreement** | Rabbi Yehuda against the Sages (חכמים = the Gemara's רבנן) |
| Does the anonymous mishnah follow the Rabbis? (45a:10-11) | Attribution question | Rav harmonises. He does not dispute anyone |
| Standing grain (45a:14) | Same ruling, different verse | Rabbi Yehuda: בשדה. Rabbis: כי תקצור קצירך בשדך |
| Floated sheaves (45a:15) | Same ruling, different derivation | Rabbi Yehuda: בשדך. Rabbis: the contrast בשדה/בשדך, which Rabbi Yehuda rejects as a method |
| Floated-sheaves teaching | **Transmission** | Rabbi Abahu in the name of Rabbi Elazar |

The earlier c9 ("Yehuda opposes the distinction") is right only as a
disagreement about method. The one legal dispute sits in 45a:10, which the
input did not include.

## Wider text (context only)

- 45a:16-17: Rabbi Yirmeya asks a dilemma. Rav Kahana answers **Rav Pappi, or
  some say Rav Zevid**. This is one event with two possible addressees, and Rav Kahana is
  citing the teaching, not receiving it from Rabbi Abahu.
- 45a:20: Rabbi Shimon ben Yehuda speaks *in the name of* Rabbi Shimon. His father,
  implied by the patronymic, gets a local placeholder and is not merged with the Rabbi Yehuda of 45a:10.
- 45b:5-6: a Tannaitic ר' אלעזר in a baraita is a different local figure from Rabbi
  Abahu's source. Do not merge them by name.
- In the Jerusalem Talmud (Peah 6:3) the "two slain" story is told of **Rav**, and in the
  Bavli (45a:23) of **Abaye**. This is a literary difference between two works, not
  evidence about either man.

## Limits

Only Sefaria texts were read: Wikisource and William Davidson. No manuscripts were checked. The
Davidson English and the Steinsaltz Hebrew come from one editorial project. The
Vilna Tosafot record says there are no Tosafot for this chapter. Several later
works that quote 45a:14-15 were not read. No historical identity is asserted, and
all graph proposals are provisional.
