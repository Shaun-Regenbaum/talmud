# Working notes (saved early; dossier.json is the deliverable)

- F-parallel: Menachot 98a:18-19 carries the SAME exchange "אמר ליה רב חסדא לרב המנונא ואמרי לה רב המנונא לרב חסדא רבי לטעמיה דאמר על בסמוך דתניא ..." in a different topic (shewbread, 2 rows of 7). Sotah 37a:9-10 cites the same baraita (כדתניא) with no amora, as a support for "מאי על על בסמוך" said in Rabbi's own name. So the Hisda/Hamnuna attribution is a recurring unit; the baraita citation can be the anonymous editorial voice.
- F-sifra: Sifra Emor ch.13:8 (Venice 1545) has the Hanina baraita with name "חנינא בן עכינאי" (ayin, not het), no "הקב"ה", and no Hisda/Hamnuna. Name-form variant; keep as source variant.
- F-tosafot78b: Tosafot Menachot 78b frames 62a as a dispute "רבי ורבנן" over whether על means בסמוך or ממש.
- F-rashi: Rashi 62a:13:3 applies the proof back to "על שני הכבשים על בסמוך" (commentary, not text).
- F-rabbi-name: "Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi" appears only in unbolded William Davidson English (editorial).
- F-recur: exact search "רב חסדא לרב המנונא ואמרי לה" -> Bavli segments Menachot 62a:12, 62a:19, 98a:19, Shabbat 97a:17 (scope: this exact-phrase search only).
- F-yalkut: Yalkut Shimoni Torah 644 (Torat Emet) has "חֲנַנְיָה בֶּן חֲכִינַאי" and "וְהָא בָּעִינַן "עַל", רַבִּי לְטַעְמֵהּ" with NO Hisda/Hamnuna; also 62a:19 parallel anonymous. Anthology abbreviation -> not proof of a Bavli variant.
- F-malbim: four views (tanna kamma, R. Yosi b. HaMeshullam, Hanina b. Akhinai, Rabbi).
- F-tos-sotah: Tosafot Sotah 37a links Abba Shaul; R. Meir & R. Yehuda "על ממש" (Tosafot inference "ונראה").
