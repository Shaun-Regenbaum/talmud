# Working notes (saved as established) — challenge-08, Chagigah 3a:4

1. Davidson English 3a:4: "sons of the sister of Rabbi Yoḥanan</b> ben Gudgeda" — "ben Gudgeda" is PLAIN (editor), not bold. The Aramaic second report says only דרבי יוחנן.
2. Davidson English "sit before</b> the Sages" — "the Sages" plain = editor's resolution of plural קמייהו. Steinsaltz Hebrew: "לפניהם, לפני החכמים" (same editorial work, not independent).
3. Community English 3a:4 "sit before him"; Goldschmidt German "sich vor ihn setzten" — singular, i.e. Rabbi. Ein Yaakov (Glick) Hebrew reads קמיה (singular). Sherira epistle (Oxford 1888 ed.) reads קמיה.
4. Rav Sherira Gaon Epistle 1:38 (Seder HaChachamim, Oxford 1888): "בני אחתיה דר' יוחנן בן גודגדא ואמרי ליה בני ברתיה דר' יוחנן בן גודגדא" — reversed order, full name in both. Also adds ותוספתא. Community translation there mistranslates ואמרי ליה as the daughter's sons "said".
5. Davidson Hebrew 3a:5 "וכולה תלמודא" vs Wikisource input "וכולה הש\"ס". Ein Yaakov "ותוספתא וכולי גמרא"; Sherira "ותוספתא וכוליה תלמודא".
6. Tosefta Terumot 1:1 and Yerushalmi Terumot 1:1: R. Yehuda reports "בניו של ר' יוחנן בן גודגדה שהיו חרשים" — his SONS, deaf (חרשים), Jerusalem purities. Tosefta Kifshutah: there = his actual sons in Temple times; Bavli = grandsons in Rabbi's time; different stories; Yuchasin tried to harmonize.
7. Guggenheimer footnote on Yerushalmi summarizes Bavli as "his grandsons, the sons of his normal daughter" — drops the sister alternative.
8. Rashi 3a:4 no comment on the story; Rashi 3a:5 הלכתא = משניות. Rashi 3a:6 calls them "הנך תרי אילמי".
9. Petach Einayim / Ben Yehoyada / Seder HaDorot: kabbalistic tradition (Rama of Fano from R. Israel Saruk) that the two were reincarnations (gilgul) of Eldad and Medad. Interpretive/mystical, not a historical identity.
10. Yalkut Shimoni on Torah 356:3 (Torat Emet): only "בני ברתיה דרבי יוחנן בן גודגדא, דכל אימת..." — no ואמרי לה sister clause; "הוו עיילי קמיה" singular, no ויתבי; adds ותוספתא.
11. Ein Yaakov (Daat ed.) 1:2: has both reports; "ויתבי קמיה" singular; "(ותוספתא)" in parentheses.
12. Rabbeinu Chananel on 3a: summarizes "הנהו תרי אילמי דבעא רבי רחמי עלייהו ואתפתחו" — no kinship, no neighborhood; adds ותוספתא. (A paraphrase; not evidence his text lacked the clauses.)
13. Missed relation in first reading: בשבבותיה דרבי = the pair lived in Rabbi's neighbourhood (neighbour relation), no claim recorded.
14. Missed attribution: ותניא = anonymous baraita source for the exclusions; 3a:6 Mar Zutra and Rav Ashi answer the objection (wider context).
15. Learning from Rabbi (teacher-student) is NOT stated; Guggenheimer ("from his lectures") and Sherira (Tosefta taught in Rabbi's study house) interpret it.
16. c1 patronymic claim is branch-restricted to daughter_report in first reading; the name is in the text regardless of which kinship report is right -> should be shared.
17. MEY Terumot 1:1:48 says "שני בניו היו חירשים" citing Tosefta; saved Tosefta text says only בניו (no number), Yerushalmi says כולן.
